## 👨‍🎓 About Me
I am a first-year PhD student at University of Auckland [Industrial AI Group](https://uoa-iai.github.io/#people), supervised by ​Dr. [Yuqian Lu](https://uoa-iai.github.io/authors/yuqian-lu/), with co-supervision from Dr. [Andrew McDaid](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=zvwTqpQAAAAJ&hl=en) and Prof. [Bruce MacDonald](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=sWz5--8AAAAJ&hl=en). My research focuses on robot skill learning for long-horizon robotic assembly tasks.

Prior to this, I earned my ​Master of Engineering in Robotics​ from the National University of Singapore (NUS), where I conducted research under the guidance of ​Prof. [Chee Meng Chew](https://sites.google.com/site/cmchewhome/home)​ and ​Prof. [Hongliang Guo](https://scholar.google.com.sg/citations?user=vR9kUw8AAAAJ&hl=en), and completed my ​Bachelor of Engineering in Aircraft Design​ at Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU).

## 🤖 Research Interests

- Long Horizon Robotic Assembly
- Robot Skill Learning
- Multi-Robot System
